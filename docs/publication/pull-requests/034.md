# EDI-40 Raise and bind a research board's decision card when the board is armed

## What it changes

The commits, in order:

- Raise and bind a research board's decision card when the board is armed
- Refuse a board card that would reopen or replace its task's decision
- Keep every decision of a task and raise a new number after an answer
- Resolve decisions by number and keep board card bindings
- Keep one open decision per task when raising by number
- Tell the scout when a board answer is refused
- Tell the scout of board answer refusals from settlement
- Record scout refusal notices from settlement in the decision record

## How it was verified

The repository's build and tests, `./mvnw verify`, passed before the merge, with the native build where the change could reach the native image, and CI ran on the pull request.
