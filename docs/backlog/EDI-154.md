## Goal

The turn report and the sitrep show each worker whose question the primary has not answered, so "nothing waiting" can never be printed while a worker waits.

## Context

On 2 November 2026 a worker waited an hour on a question the primary never saw, while every turn report said nothing was waiting: the decision zone lists the captain's decisions, not workers waiting on the primary.
The question's key and its resolving steer are already stored.

## Acceptance criteria

- The decision zone of the turn report gets a "Waiting on EDI" line per open worker question: the task's title, the question in a few words and how long it has waited.
- The sitrep page shows the same, in the same zone.
- The line disappears once a steer that resolves its key is acknowledged.
- "Nothing waiting on you" is printed only when there are neither open decisions nor open worker questions.
- Tests cover the line's presence, its removal, and the empty zone.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Soft and silent failures are covered by hard rules that are non-negotiable and non-bypassable.
- The turn report is an attention list: only what needs attention in the session belongs in it.
- Captain-facing text speaks in outcomes, never in internal labels.
