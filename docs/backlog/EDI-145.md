## Goal

Each validation gets an independent review: a fresh, read-only reviewer session that EDI launches and supervises reads the change against the captain's intent and the project's review rules, reports its findings, and pauses on a usage limit instead of failing.

## Context

The part of no-mistakes EDI really relies on is its reviewer; the rest is replaced slice by slice.
Every agent failure measured from 21 to 27 October 2026 was a usage limit (9 of 13 failed runs), and the pipeline failed the run each time, once leaving a run failed overnight; EDI already detects a worker's limit stop and resumes it at the reset.
The captain chose a reviewer that runs as a supervised EDI session through `WorkerRuntime` (Q4 A).
This item adds the `Review` stage to the validation built in slice 1, which must have merged first: it is the item filed from `slice-1.md`.

## Before starting

- Slice 1 has merged, and EDI-105's revision of this item holds.
- Confirm the launch and guard classes against the code as it then stands; EDI-122, EDI-123 and EDI-125 changed what a session EDI launches may do.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new `LaunchKind.REVIEW` launches a reviewer session through `WorkerRuntime` on the dispatch rules' `pipeline` route, in the validation's pinned worktree, with read-only tools, under the same guards as a worker.
- The reviewer reads the diff from the base, the pipeline intent (`PipelineIntent`), the brief's craft rules and the project's review rules, and reports with `edi review report --findings -`, JSON the core validates against `Finding`; a report that does not validate is refused, naming the field.
- The project's review rules live in `.edi/review.md` with path-scoped sections; EDI's own is seeded from `.no-mistakes.yaml`'s `review.path_instructions` and `document.instructions`, so the documentation rules no-mistakes' document step checked are part of the review.
- The findings are stored and reach the worker as one inbox message; an `ask-captain` finding reaches the worker as a decision to raise, never one it may answer.
- A reviewer that hits a usage limit is resumed at the reset by the supervision round, and its validation stays running, not failed (a test on a recorded transcript with a limit stop).
- The reviewer's tokens are counted from its transcript into `task_cost`'s pipeline columns, and its rounds and findings into the records EDI-97 defines.
- The reviewer never runs in, or resumes, the worker's own session.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Q4 A (the captain, 29 October 2026): the reviewer is a supervised EDI session, a review launch through `WorkerRuntime`, read-only.
- Q2 A, with the captain's condition that the worker fixing findings must be safe and unbiased: the reviewer is always a fresh session independent of the worker, and the review ruleset is explicit and versioned (craft rules, `.edi/review.md`, decision-log rules).
- The reviewer's route comes from the dispatch rules' `pipeline` entry, never the harness default ([pipeline-agent](../decisions/pipeline-agent.md)).
- No seventh port: the reviewer runs through `WorkerRuntime`.

## Research and measurements

- Review: 182 calls, 64.9M weighted tokens (28%); document: 81 calls, 16.0M (7%), which this review absorbs.
- All nine `claude exited` failures in the window were session limits.
