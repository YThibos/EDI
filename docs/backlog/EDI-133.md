## Goal

The turn report (`edi report --turn`) becomes the session's attention list, and the overview stays with `edi sitrep` and the console's sitrep page.

## Context

The captain found the boxed turn reports cumbersome: with several parallel tasks, even the "what changed" form means a lot of scrolling.
The sitrep is the overview of the state of the work; the turn report is the talk between the captain and the primary, for the primary to forward to workers or to dispatch work.

## Acceptance criteria

- The turn report keeps what needs the captain now: open decisions, ready-for-review merge requests with their full address, blockers and failures that need the captain, and this turn's items (done and next) given on standard input.
- A decision card is shown in full in the turn it is raised or changed; in later turns it is one line (the D number, the title and the recommended option), so every open decision still appears in every report (I-19) without repeating the card.
- The turn report drops the ongoing and parked lines, the heavy-runs builds line and anything else that is state rather than a call for the captain; they stay in `edi sitrep` and on the console page, unchanged.
- A turn with nothing for the captain renders nothing, or one line.
- The stop hook and the anchor's "Ending a turn" follow, with the anchor under its byte budget; the sitrep skill and edi-voice are updated where they describe the report.
- The design is recorded in `docs/decisions` (the report as attention list versus the sitrep as overview), and the new layout is pinned with presenter tests, including a report with long-open decisions.
