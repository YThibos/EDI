## Goal

Revise the design of EDI's own validation pipeline on EDI-74's task record and plans, keeping the research's measurements and the captain's answers, and update the six slice items in place so each can be released as written.

## Context

The captain asked for a researcher to design how EDI could run its own validation pipeline (review, test, document, lint, push, pull or merge request, CI watch, and gates that ask the captain) instead of driving the external no-mistakes tool.
The research ([EDI-105 research](research/EDI-105-validation-pipeline.md)) found that every failure mode measured from 21 to 27 October 2026 comes from a part of no-mistakes that EDI does not need or already does better, and recommended six slices, cut over one project at a time after a shadow period, saving about half of the pipeline's tokens.
On 29 October 2026 the captain answered the research board: D47 A, build all six slices, and A to each of the four design questions, with a condition on Q2.
The six slices are on the backlog as their own items, parked behind this one, because EDI-74's lifecycle work (EDI-85, EDI-86, EDI-70, EDI-67, EDI-88 and EDI-89) and EDI-52's slices (EDI-69 and the console items) change much of what they build on.
The shortcut that bridges the gap, EDI-104, has landed ([pipeline-steps](../decisions/pipeline-steps.md)).

## Research and measurements

- The measurements to keep are in [the EDI-105 research](research/EDI-105-validation-pipeline.md): outcomes of 93 runs (58 completed, 13 failed, 17 cancelled, 2 CI monitors interrupted, 3 running), the pipeline's share of 36% of a costed task's weighted tokens, 2.59M weighted per run, a median 55-minute run with a median 16 minutes parked at gates, and the per-step token table with the test step at 45%.
- The failure modes with evidence: a rebase followed by a failed fix agent strands the gate (two ships stalled 2 hours 31 minutes each); fix and step agents exit on usage limits and fail the run (nine of 13 failures); the CI step waits on Actions that never run (40 of 67 CI steps); the CI monitor rebases an open pull request; a daemon restart interrupts the CI watch; pushes use whichever account is active.
- The overlap with the EDI-74 and EDI-52 items is tabled in the research's section "What changes before this is picked up".
