## Goal

Revise the research on EDI's own validation pipeline against the new task record and plans, keeping its measurements, and put the captain's decision D47 again.

## Context

The captain asked for a researcher to design how EDI could run its own validation pipeline (review, test, document, lint, push, pull or merge request, CI watch, and gates that ask the captain) instead of driving the external no-mistakes tool.
The first research ([EDI-105 research](research/EDI-105-validation-pipeline.md)) found that every failure mode measured since 21 October 2026 comes from a part of no-mistakes that EDI does not need or already does better, and recommended six slices, cut over one project at a time after a shadow period, saving about half of the pipeline's tokens.
A research board with five questions was put to the captain; he held decision D47 and the research, then parked the board until the lifecycle upgrade has landed, so that the new workings are taken into account.
The shortcut that bridges the gap, EDI-104, has landed: the pipeline skips its rebase and, on projects whose CI does not run, its CI step, and the worker merges the base before each run.

- Research only: the report authorises nothing, and no code, skill or record outside the report changes.

## Research and measurements

- The measurements to keep are in [the EDI-105 research](research/EDI-105-validation-pipeline.md): outcomes of 93 runs (58 completed, 13 failed, 17 cancelled, 2 CI monitors interrupted, 3 running), the pipeline's share of 36% of a costed task's weighted tokens, 2.59M weighted per run, a median 55-minute run with a median 16 minutes parked at gates, and the per-step token table with the test step at 45%.
- The failure modes with evidence: a rebase followed by a failed fix agent strands the gate (two ships stalled 2 hours 31 minutes each); fix and step agents exit on usage limits and fail the run (nine of 13 failures); the CI step waits on Actions that never run (40 of 67 CI steps); the CI monitor rebases an open pull request; a daemon restart interrupts the CI watch; pushes use whichever account is active.
- The first design: `Validation`, `ValidationStage` (BaseSync, Review, Verify, Publish, Checks) and `Finding` in the domain; a reviewer as a supervised session; no seventh port, so the `Pipeline` port goes at the cut-over, leaving five.
