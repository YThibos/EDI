## Goal

Every Monday the primary runs one gardening round that gathers the week's numbers, surfaces slips and silent failures, proposes hard rules and prunings, tidies hygiene within standing rules, and puts it all on one Lavish board for the captain.

## Context

The captain's words: self-improvement is key to higher quality and standards, a craftsman's core value, and the introspecting mentality should be baked into the way of working.
The weekly upkeep check (Renovate, tool versions) is the start; gardening takes it further.
Cadence: Monday, after the weekly usage budget resets at 09:00, so a round is not interrupted by weekly limits.
Slip signals the captain set: his pushback in general, and two literal markers he puts in front of a quoted piece of the primary's text: "(!)" when he disagrees, pushes back or marks an issue, and "(?)" when he softly questions what happened or was done; plus the primary's own triggers.
The week of 26 October 2026 is the baseline: `~/.edi/data/introspect/primary-ops.py` and its baseline file, and the captain's numbers board.

## Acceptance criteria

- A `gardening` skill and its script, built on the introspect script of EDI-155 and the rule telemetry of EDI-164, merged with the existing weekly upkeep check, offered by the session-start digest on Monday after 09:00 local time.
- The script gathers, with no model tokens: the routine counts against the previous week; `edi rules` (fired, never fired, fired often); slip signals: the captain's messages carrying "(!)" or "(?)", his corrections ("you misunderstood", "why is", "I didn't see", "again", "I said", a restated rule, "wait, I meant"), the primary's own admissions of a slip, decisions answered with a note that overrides the options, merges made against a warning, worker questions unanswered over 10 minutes, gates parked over 30 minutes, requests changing over an hour, captain-word and safety-check refusals, steers never acknowledged, review rounds per request, defects found after a merge; repeated hand sequences (the same commands chained five times or more); hygiene drift: tasks held without reason over two weeks, orphaned worktrees and branches, backlog items untouched over four weeks, story files disagreeing with the tracker, TODO or FIXME on closed tickets, decision records naming removed code, the memory budget.
- Headline numbers are outcomes: the captain's corrections and markers, question-to-answer time, review rounds per request, done-to-landed time, defects after merge; call counts support them.
- The round produces one Lavish board: the week against the previous; the top three slips with evidence and a drafted backlog item each, as a hard rule, for approve, adjust or reject; rules to retire or prove with a test; hygiene done under standing rules and hygiene needing the captain's word; the spark inbox sorted (EDI-166).
- Approved drafts go upstream in one batch through `edi-backlog-item`; nothing becomes a rule without the captain's word.
- Tests cover the gathering on an invented week, each slip detector, and the board's data.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Soft and silent failures are covered by hard rules that are non-negotiable and non-bypassable.
- Monday, after the 09:00 weekly reset.
- The captain's markers "(!)" and "(?)" are slip signals, together with his pushback and the primary's own triggers.
- Automation detects, measures and drafts; making or retiring a rule and choosing a direction stay with the captain and the primary.
