## Goal

A task whose work is finished and fully handled is retired by the core itself, so the sitrep's zones never carry finished work that only waits for the primary to remember `edi cleanup`.

## Context

On 2 November 2026 the sitrep's Ongoing view listed three finished research scouts beside four running tasks, one of them looking like a duplicate of a running ship.
The primary retired them by hand only after the captain asked.
EDI-65 moves a delivered scout from Ongoing to "report ready", but nothing retires it once its report has been promoted, or its done accepted and its decisions answered, so it would still linger in another zone.
The captain asks for hard rules wherever a failure would otherwise be soft or silent: here, a forgotten cleanup.

## Acceptance criteria

- A scout is retired automatically, as `edi cleanup` does today, when its report exists, every decision it raised is answered, and either it has been promoted to a ship or the captain's answer or a stated rule accepts its done; its report and board stay.
- A ship is retired automatically when all its work has landed, as EDI-23 already does on a merge; the same rule covers a ship delivered by report or local branch once its delivery is confirmed.
- A retirement that would discard unlanded work is refused and shown, never forced, and nothing is discarded without the captain's word.
- The retirement is recorded in the task's timeline with its reason, and the turn report mentions it once.
- A task stays in "report ready" only while something is still open on it: an unanswered decision, an unpromoted report the captain has not seen, or an unconfirmed delivery.
- Tests cover each retirement path, the refusal for unlanded work, and a scout with an open decision that stays.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Soft and silent failures are covered by hard rules that are non-negotiable and non-bypassable.
- Unlanded work is never discarded without the captain's word.
- The turn report is an attention list; the sitrep is the overview.
