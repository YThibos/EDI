## Goal

The primary reads a worker's whole last status and open question with one command, instead of reading its terminal pane.

## Context

`edi worker list` cuts each status to one line, so in the week of 26 October 2026 the primary read workers' panes 47 times to see a full question or status.

## Acceptance criteria

- `edi worker show <task>` prints the worker's state, model and effort, its last status in full, its open question with its key if it has one, its steers with their acknowledgement, and its pipeline stage.
- It reads only the store, never the pane.
- Tests cover a working, a needs-decision and a finished worker.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Judge workers by their structured state, never by a pane alone.

## Read first

- The `edi worker` commands in `edi-cli/src/main/java/io/github/ythibos/edi/cli/`, the worker store.

## How to verify

- `./mvnw verify`, then the native quick build and its smoke test.
- By hand: `edi worker show` on a worker with a long needs-decision prints the whole question.
