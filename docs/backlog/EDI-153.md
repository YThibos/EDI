## Goal

A worker's needs-decision stays open until a steer that resolves its key is acknowledged, and the core raises it again to the primary at a fixed interval until then, whatever happened to the first wake.

## Context

On 2 November 2026 a needs-decision wake was taken by a stray `edi wait` and counted acknowledged; nothing raised it again, and the worker waited an hour.
Since EDI-158 a wake counts acknowledged only once the primary acts on its task, a later wake for that task is printed, or the session it was printed to ends cleanly ([wakes-and-turn-end](../decisions/wakes-and-turn-end.md)), so a question printed and let pass is still never raised again.
The question itself carries a key that a steer with `--resolves` answers, so the core already knows when a question is still open.
This item is the safety net behind any cause of a lost wake, not only the one seen.

## Acceptance criteria

- A needs-decision whose key no acknowledged `--resolves` steer has answered is raised again as a reminder wake every 10 minutes, naming the worker, the key and how long it has waited.
- The interval is a setting with 10 minutes as its default.
- The reminder stops the moment a resolving steer is recorded, and also when the worker reports another state.
- A reminder is never raised while the captain is away under a mandate that lets workers wait, and resumes on return; presence quiet still reminds the primary, since the primary answers worker questions in quiet.
- `edi health` lists each open worker question with its age.
- Tests cover the reminder, its stop on a resolving steer, its stop on a new worker state, and the interval setting.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Soft and silent failures are covered by hard rules that are non-negotiable and non-bypassable.
- Tracked tests use invented task ids and keys.
