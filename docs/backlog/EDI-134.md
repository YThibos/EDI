## Goal

`edi merged` reports one outcome after the removal of a task's isolated copy ends, or says the removal is under way, and never reports a half-removed copy as unlanded work.

## Context

Twice on 28 October 2026 `edi merged` answered "not cleaned up yet" and listed every tracked file as "not committed" while the core was still removing the task's copy.
Seconds later the copy was gone and the task was done.
The report took the files already removed for uncommitted work.

## Acceptance criteria

- A failing test first reproduces it: the merge's cleanup is asked while the removal of the copy is under way, and it reports the missing files as unlanded work.
- The merge's cleanup reports one outcome once the removal ends, or says that the removal is under way.
- A copy that is partly removed is never reported as holding uncommitted or unlanded work.
- A copy that holds real uncommitted work is still refused and listed, as before.
- Both are pinned with tests, with time from the `Clock` port and no sleeping.
