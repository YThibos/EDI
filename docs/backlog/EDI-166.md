## Goal

An idea from the captain or the primary is captured in one step with its context, without interrupting the work, and sorted at the weekly gardening, so the backlog only receives items that have been thought through.

## Context

The captain wants a place for "random idea sparks" beside the measured gardening.
Today an idea either goes straight to the backlog, half-formed, or is lost in a session.

## Acceptance criteria

- `edi spark "<text>"` stores a spark with its time, its author (captain or primary), the session, and the task or project in focus; when the captain writes a message starting "Spark:" or "spark:", the harness stores it the same way.
- `edi spark list` shows the open sparks; each can be marked backlogged (with the item's key), merged into an existing item, or dropped with a reason.
- The gardening board (EDI-165) lists the week's open sparks for sorting.
- Tests cover capture by command and by the captain's message, and each way of closing a spark.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Ideas go through the gardening before the backlog.
- EDI goes public first; this is the clear second priority.

## Read first

- The captain's message hooks from EDI-159, `claude/skills/edi-backlog-item/`, the gardening item (EDI-165).

## How to verify

- `./mvnw verify`, then the native quick build and its smoke test.
- By hand: a "Spark: ..." message appears in `edi spark list` with its context.
