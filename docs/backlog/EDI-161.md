## Goal

When a worker reports done, the core verifies its delivery, books its time and moves its ticket itself, and on the merge it moves the ticket on and cleans up, so none of these checks or bookings depends on the primary remembering them.

## Context

In the week of 26 October 2026 the primary spent about 140 tool calls on this by hand: 77 merge-request state checks, 40 JIRA calls (worklogs, transitions, sprint), and 25 checks for SNAPSHOT versions and the worked line.
On 2 November it booked worked time at the wrong hour once (the worker's times were UTC), and a ticket stayed in To Do after its merge request was ready because a transition was refused.
The captain's JIRA flow: Test in DEV when a merge request is ready for his review, TEST in TST once merged into master, Ready For Prd once tested; a ticket enters the current sprint when work starts; evergreen tickets get worklogs only.

## Acceptance criteria

- On a ship's done the core checks, through the forge adapter: the request is open and not a draft, its head is the worker's head, its checks are green on that head, it is mergeable, and the branch adds no SNAPSHOT version; any failure goes back to the worker as a steer naming it, and the done is not accepted.
- The core reads the worked line of the task's report, converts its times correctly (stating UTC or local explicitly), and books one worklog on the task's ticket through the tracker adapter, with a comment naming the work and the request; a missing or malformed worked line is refused at done.
- The core moves the ticket to the project's "ready for review" state and adds it to the current sprint; on the merge it moves it to the project's "merged" state; evergreen tickets are never transitioned.
- The states and their transition paths come from the project's tracker configuration, not from code.
- Each step is recorded in the task's timeline, and a failed tracker call becomes a blocker in the turn report, never a silent skip.
- Tests cover a clean done, each refused done, the booking, the transitions, an evergreen ticket and a failed tracker call.

## Requirements from decisions and answers

- Soft and silent failures are covered by hard rules that are non-negotiable and non-bypassable.
- Worklog amounts are the worker's real minutes, never rounded or invented.
- The captain's JIRA status flow, and stories never in Done before production.
